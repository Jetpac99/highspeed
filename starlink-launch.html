Launch story

One flight. A very different onboard feeling.

This is not just faster internet. It is a cabin that feels more natural to use. Plans can still move. Group chats can still sparkle. Work can still be wrapped, streamed or sent before the wheels are up and while the city lights drop away. That is what makes the launch feel bigger than one route.

Virgin Atlantic has positioned Starlink as part of a more connected, "home away from home" experience in the air. The promise is simple: less waiting, less buffering and fewer moments where life has to stop just because you are flying.
